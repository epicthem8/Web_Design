Who Are We?

Responsible AI is an online resource that helps everyday people, businesses, and schools use AI thoughtfully. Most responsible AI work focuses on the companies building the models. We focus on the people using them.

Every prompt uses energy and water, shapes what others read and trust, and affects the skills of the person typing it. We explain those impacts in plain language and share practical strategies and trusted resources, so you can get the benefits of AI without the hidden costs. The habits we build now will shape how AI is used for decades. We're here to help make them good ones.
